## Production files and corrections

All eleven NPCs (`mayor`, `kid`, `farmer`, `smith`, `baker`, `fisher`, `ranger`, `weaver`, `peddler`, `twin`, `rival`) have `illustrated/{id}-source.png`, four `illustrated/{id}-{down,up,left,right}.webp` sheets and `illustrated/{id}-manifest.json`. Each optimized sheet has four 64x96 pixel frames, displayed as 16x24 logical pixels with smooth filtering and a bottom-center anchor. Source bounds and correction flags are recorded in each manifest.

- Kid and peddler: the generated west row contained east-facing walking poses. The output west sheet mirrors the complete clean east sheet (`--mirror-left`).
- Baker and weaver: generated side rows arrived east then west. Source rows are swapped into canonical west then east (`--swap-sides`).
- The other seven NPCs' directional rows are consumed in their original order.
- Hero and Gran were reprocessed through the silhouette-aware helper to restore complete row silhouettes and a shared scale across animation frames. Hero uses its three clean source rows only (`--three-rows --crop-height=954`); east still mirrors west at runtime. The unusable original fourth source row remains excluded.

The slicer finds the largest connected alpha silhouettes rather than cutting the image at exact quarter heights. This fixes the prior Gran west-row contamination from the next row's heads and resulting smaller apparent scale. The broad smith and pigtail child retain their natural silhouette within the common 16x24 frame; no limbs are clipped and no character is stretched to fill a frame.
